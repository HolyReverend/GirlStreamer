# OI-110: Validate donation variance and early finances

- **Type:** Validation
- **Status:** Open
- **Area:** Economy
- **Issue:** Tune starting reserves, rent, expenses, prices and early income, including introductory streams and growth. Test donation sizes, distribution, frequency and caps, direct loyalty effects and protection against bad-luck streaks. A guaranteed top-up is unapproved. Preserve the agreed income-before-expenses rule.
- **Return trigger:** When running the first multi-day economy prototype.
- **Close when:** Validate early self-sufficiency and reasonable variance across seeds and both styles; record approved values.
