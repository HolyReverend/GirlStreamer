# OI-104: Validate the integrated game concept

- **Type:** Proposal
- **Status:** Open
- **Area:** Game design
- **Issue:** The proposed scope combines one arcade, a short stream, contextual chat, questions and donations, rest, promotion, simple expenses, two test outfits and two play styles. Test divided attention, readability, causes of audience support, financial viability of both styles, meaningful preparation, and whether the heroine and rewards sustain interest.
- **Return trigger:** When planning the first prototype with a day cycle and economy.
- **Close when:** Agree on scope and record human playtest findings and remaining decisions.
