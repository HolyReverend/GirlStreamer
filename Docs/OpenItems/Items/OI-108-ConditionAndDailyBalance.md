# OI-108: Validate day planning, fatigue and recovery

- **Type:** Validation
- **Status:** Open
- **Area:** Day cycle
- **Issue:** Decide initial values (8/8 is only an example), repeatability, activity prices, preparation duration, promotion returns, recovery, arcade fatigue penalties and question-time modifiers. Test variety without mandatory rest or skipped streams, dominant routines or ignoring penalties; check training cost and both styles. Consider an unused-AP reminder and normal-price quick rest before starting.
- **Return trigger:** When prototyping daily preparation and condition.
- **Close when:** Play a multi-day prototype, record tradeoffs and agree on values and preparation UI behavior.
- **Sources:** [ActionPointsAndCondition](../../GameDesign/Mechanics/ActionPointsAndCondition.md), [CoreGameplayLoop](../../GameDesign/Mechanics/CoreGameplayLoop.md), [ConceptDesign](../../GameDesign/ConceptDesign.md).
