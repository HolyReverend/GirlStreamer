# OI-105: Validate channel paths and the mixed style

- **Type:** Proposal
- **Status:** Open
- **Area:** Audience
- **Issue:** Compare hardcore play (mastery, excitement, records, challenge and durable support; practice and game preparation) with social play (charisma, flirting, answers and reactions; communication and outfit preparation). Consider a mixed style limited by divided training time. Test whether achievement-driven growth and recurring support compensate for narrower reach, whether neutral skips are appropriate, and whether both styles remain financially viable.
- **Return trigger:** When planning a prototype with long-term channel growth.
- **Close when:** Choose or reject mixed specialization and document the tested tradeoffs for both paths.
