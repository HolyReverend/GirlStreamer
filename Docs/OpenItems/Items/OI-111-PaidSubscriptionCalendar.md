# OI-111: Specify paid subscription rules and calendar

- **Type:** Question
- **Status:** Open
- **Area:** Economy
- **Issue:** Determine conversion and renewal rates from impression and loyalty, exact paid-period boundaries and renewal processing date. Keep one payment per purchase or renewal; due income precedes expenses on the same day, and one bad or skipped stream does not erase a paid period.
- **Return trigger:** Before implementing paid subscriptions.
- **Close when:** Document and verify purchase, renewal, expiry, missed-stream and rent-day cases.
- **Sources:** [Economy](../../GameDesign/Mechanics/Economy.md), [AudienceAndDonations](../../GameDesign/Mechanics/AudienceAndDonations.md), [ConceptDesign](../../GameDesign/ConceptDesign.md).
