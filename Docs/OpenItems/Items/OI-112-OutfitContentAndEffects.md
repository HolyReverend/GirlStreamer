# OI-112: Decide outfit content and audience effects

- **Type:** Proposal
- **Status:** Open
- **Area:** Outfits
- **Issue:** Choose specific outfits, effects and prices. Consider whole outfits for initial production versus freely mixing pieces, and separating appearance from play specialization. Outfits can shape expectations without dictating behavior or loyalty.
- **Return trigger:** When planning the first purchasable outfit set.
- **Close when:** Approve the content list, progression and audience effects.
